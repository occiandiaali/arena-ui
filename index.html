Frequently asked questions

What is a magic link?

A single-use sign-in link sent to your email. Clicking it proves it's you, so no password is needed.

Do I need to create an account first?

No. Enter your email below and your account is created automatically the first time you sign in.

I didn't receive the email. What now?

Check your spam folder, then request a new link. Links expire after a short time and can only be used once.

Is it free?

Yes, joining and browsing members is free.
